Terms & Conditions

Plain-language terms for using Olvra Boost's wallet, social media boosting, and Rent Number services.

🧾 Your Wallet

  • Your wallet is funded by card or bank transfer and is credited automatically once a payment is confirmed.
  • Wallet balance has no cash value outside Olvra Boost and cannot be withdrawn — it can only be spent on services within the app.
  • All charges (boosting orders, number rentals) are deducted from your wallet the moment an order is placed, before the order is sent to any provider.
  • If you believe you were charged in error, contact support from the app with your order ID — most issues are resolved by checking the order's status and provider response first.

📈 Boost Account (social media orders)

  • Orders are fulfilled through third-party delivery providers, not by Olvra Boost directly — delivery speed and final quality can vary by service.
  • You may only order for accounts, pages, or links you own or have explicit permission to promote.
  • If an order can't fully deliver, whatever portion wasn't delivered is refunded to your wallet automatically.
  • Refill and cancel are available only on services that support them, as shown on that service's page — not guaranteed on every order.
  • Engagement quality and permanence (e.g. follower retention) are not guaranteed and can vary by platform and service.

📱 Rent Number (virtual phone numbers)

  • Numbers are supplied by a third-party SMS-verification provider. Olvra Boost marks up the provider's live price — you always see the final price before paying.
  • Time limit: every rented number has an expiry countdown. If no SMS code arrives before it expires, the order closes automatically and is refunded to your wallet in full — no need to contact support.
  • Once a code is delivered, the number is considered successfully fulfilled and is no longer eligible for a refund, even if you didn't get to use it in time.
  • A number can occasionally come back reported/banned by the service you're verifying on — this refunds automatically the same way a timeout does.
  • Numbers are for receiving one-time verification codes only. Olvra Boost does not support sending outgoing SMS or calls from a rented number, and numbers must only be used for lawful sign-ups and verifications.
  • A rented number is single-use for the service it was purchased for — it is not a personal phone line and should not be shared publicly as one.
  • By using Rent Number, you agree to these terms in addition to the general terms on this page — you'll also see a short version of this before your first rental.
